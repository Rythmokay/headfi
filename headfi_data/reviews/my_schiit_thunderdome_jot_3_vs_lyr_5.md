## Reviews & Audio Impressions

### Review #1 by  (Rating: 5.00 star(s))

I have wanted to do this for a long time. Comparing two amplifiers I could buy for use with my many headphones. Thinking about all the things that really matter in my day to day testing and listening with headphones. Easy to drive, hard to drive, dynamics, planars, AMT drivers etc.

I have been buying and using many vendors amps and dacs, but one in particular, Schiit Audio recently announced two new headphone amplifies that really caught me by surprise. Their new Jotenheim 3 and Lyr 5.

The Jotenheim 3 is a totally solid state, fully discrete headphone amplifier with only 10 dB of negative feedback, Continuity A, direct coupled, tons of power, nice ALPS RK27 balanced pot, balanced and SE. Price is $499.

The Lyr 5 is a hybrid tube amp with a 6SN7 type of vacuum tube, tons of power a fancy new switching design called Fusion Architecture, where you can switch from tube gain to solid state mosfet gain mode with the press of a button. When in SS mode, the tube is totally turned off, HV and heaters. Two amps in one. Price is $799.

So there is a $300 delta here. You can plug a tube into Jot 3, so if your looking for a tube amp, the choice for you is simple or you would think. Unless you need gobs of power there are lower cost amps from Schiit, like the Valhalla 3 and Vali 3. Hmmm.

But for my Thunderdome I have these requirements:

1. A superb well-designed desktop AIO with the ability to work with external devices both XLR and RCA connections.

2. The ability to house an internal good quality dac.

3. Tons of power to drive most any headphone out there, except electrostatics.

4. No need to know if I am in Class A or AB

5. Solid, dependable "lab" type amp that would be a good baseline to test and listen with.

6. All the legacy and modern outputs, SE, XLR, 4.4mm

7. No need for PEQ. Loudness or a fancy phone app.

8. No need any out if the mainstream stuff like Halo, multiple filters.

9. Low output impedance please.

10. A vol pot that has smooth decent tracking.

Fussy, yup. Old School. Kiss as best possible. I want the amp to last, be as good as it was on day one hundreds of days down the road. I don't have a need for a pre-amp function. Built like a brick Schiit House. Cost effective.

Ok so thats me. I even didnt ask for VU meters. Just a lab amp that works, and very well day in and day out.

So lets look at the two contenders in this exercise: Jot 2 / Lyr 5

Disclaimer: Either of these amps are great and do many folks just fine. This is just picking one or the other based on my needs and requirements. Which is your amp is decided by YOU,

Jot 3 is the latest in the Jot lineage. $499 Totally solid state, discrete amp.

Lyr 5 is the latest in the Lyr lineage. $799. Hybrid tube and switchable SS tube mode via tube sounding Mosfets.

Delta is $300.

Both are pretty much total re-vamps of what they were. New stuff, improvements, new cases, new software even.

But is it really needed for YOU ?

With Lyr 5 you’re getting the latest version of Forkbeard. Nice but I don’t need this stuff. Yes, I appreciate it and do use FB in my 2-ch setup where it makes sense for me. But for headphone testing at my dedicated listening bench/desk I am at arm's reach and want to touch and spin the knobs and move the switches and/or press the buttons. With it you can tell in real time if you’re in class a or entering into class ab land. Ok nice but why? Even Jason states you would be surprised at how much your in class A even with harder to drive loads. So theres that. And if your seeing your into Class AB, what do you do? Cry, laugh turn down the volume ??? See…for me it’s a don’t care. Having a MJ3 that is in class A all the time I never had an issue with not having enough power to drive any headphone. Another nicety with Lyr FB is ability to get system health stuff like over temps and over current. Ok. As an aside I never left Class A with Jot 3 using the FB app or with Jot 3 with my Class A "ear" detector. Smile.

With Lyr 5, you're adding a second transformer, one for the HV rails and one for the tube heaters. This adds weight and cost. Then a really nice motorized ladder relay volume control, nice but more cost. Then throw in a tube, cost again. So you can see why a Lyr 5 is $799. More stuff, a new pot scheme, a tube, included ForkBeard, two transformers. While the more simpler Jot 3 is only $499, still $280 more than Midgard (my reference amp prior to Jot 3). Both for what they bring to the table are very good and IMO priced right. But in my use case some of the stuff is not needed.

And they both perform as good or better than other amps costing much more, yup that’s my story and I am …..

Using Lyr 5 with FB I never saw the amp go out of Class A, even with hard to drive cans like DCA E3, Expanse, Hedd GT 2’s. Tons of power with both.

One person posted that he didn’t want to look at another screen, referencing FB after a day working with screens! I totally understand this. I love VU meters and displays, but simple is good as well. On my listening table, I reach and touch things, I really don’t need a phone or app to play with. As neat as it is, for the 2 ch yup makes sense and I do like it there.

Then there is sonics:

Now sonically you would think there is a BIG difference. Well, there is and there is not….good English eh? I have spent hours listening with both, all the outputs over multiple of headphones. Even the guys at Schiit often state when a new thing is coming out, "this is the best thing we have ever done"...well thats nice but its still subjective IMO. And thats not bad. We all hear differently.

With Lyr 5 you have to deal with vacuum tubes. You can chase the NOS tube train, spend mega-bucks or he happy with the stock tube. Well how many of you are happy with the stock tube!! Ha! Most buy 1 or more tubes to try just to be sure!! Then in the end most sit in there small cardboard boxes. With Jot 3 this is a moot point. Ha! More $$$ in my pocket for new cans!! LOL!

I can easily get lost in the music with either. Level setting and going back and forth etc…they are very close to being the same here.

My subjective "self" thinks the Tube mode of Lyr 5 is “nicer” than the SS Lyr 5 mode, see I did not say “better”. I also think the SS mode of the Jot 3 is “nicer” than the SS mode of Lyr 5. That’s my ears. My music etc.. Maybe I should have said “different” instead of “nicer”.

Knowing Jot 3 has less power with the SE headphone output and Lyr 5 has the same power on both of its outputs…made me try all kind of combinations to see if the lower power on Jot 3 would cripple it in any way. Nope still more than enough power. And with Lyr 5 if you use the 4.4mm jack you add HALO into the mix, another 2-ohm curveball. See always something.
So for me Jot 3 wins the overall contest for my use case. And I am $300 richer! (save for Bifrost 3). Smile.

Even when I think the tube side of Lyr 5 is “nicer” than Jot 3, I listen to Lyr 5 for 20 minutes and switch to Jot 3 level set and play the same material and I smile once again and ask myself is there really a big difference here…hmmm.

Answer not enough to cough up $300. Honestly either on will make you smile a lot…Kind of boils down do you want to play with tubes and gobs of power or not. Vali3 and Vahalla 3 are available as well, but less power etc..

Jot 3 for me has power, connectivity, simplicity, balanced and SE, no Halo to add to the mix, just a simple lab amp for testing. It meets most of my requirements. And it now will replace my Midgard as "My" Lab Amp for testing.

Then there are times when doesn’t sound like anything, just a neutral sound, its close to a straight wire with gain. Isn't that what an amp is supposed to do ?

So there ya have it. Both are winners. Different flavors and designs. Both Neat offerings from Schiit.

Its good to have choices and different offering…avoid the nervosa, think it thru but don’t wait forever like a jack ass between two piles of hay!

Enjoy the music, while u can!

Alex
