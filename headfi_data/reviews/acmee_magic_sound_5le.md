## Reviews & Audio Impressions

### Review #1 by  (Rating: 4.50 star(s))

Magic Sound 5LE Decoding Headphone Bluetooth DAC/Amplifier

Price $169

ACMEE are a new company in the audiophile hobby to me but they have been around for quite a while with their DAPs and DAC/AMP they release products

That have a certain air of mystery as they don't release very often but if the Magic 5LE is anything to go by they are of high quality at an attractive price $169

The Magic Sound 5LE came with nice leather case to protect that glass back the only omission and a missed opportunity is there's no mag-safe feature to stick the perfectly formed device to your phone back.

I own a large variety of Blue tooth equipped dongle DAC/Amp coming in all shapes and sizes the Magic Sound 5LE is my favourite style, slim enough to carry in a shirt pocket, perfect for sitting on the back of your phone for usb-c connection the nearest comparison in form factor would be the more expensive Nipo A100. 

While I own many Bluetooth dongles I rarely use Bluetooth preferring a usb-c connection to squeeze the very best sound quality out of my equipment to enjoy my music.

Bluetooth

So having dismissed Bluetooth, here's my thoughts. The performance of the Magic Sound 5LE is exceptional, a rock solid connection that allowed to walk all around my house and even into the garden without any dropouts using LDAC the ES9039Q2M DAC has a beautifully smooth analogue sound signature and deserves a wired connection to really appreciate .

The Magic Sound 5LE has enormous power for such a slim device

Single-Ended: 245mw (32 Ohms)

Balanced: 980mw (32 Ohms)

More than enough to power practically all my iems and most headphones including my Hifiman HE1000 Stealth V2 and Anada Planar Headphones. Both exhibited good bass authority and sweet mids with controlled treble with enough headroom to play at unhealthy volume. There's a commendable amount of detail that doesn’t intrude on the musicality of the presentation.

This is what you expect a class leading ESS solid state device to sound like Reference yet Dynamic, smooth, natural, clean and "Balanced". There's a pleasing level of warmth with a slight bass boost the treble is clean extended and detailed with no jarring peaks or dips. I really appreciated the slower more organic approach with transients sticking around a touch longer making a more emotional connection to my music library. Some may prefer a quicker more digital clean presentation. Personally I found Magic Sound 5LE perfect for my iems and headphones never lacking dynamics it worked particularly well with my analytical/ neutral iems adding warmth and body.

The sound signature is important with sources but it’s the iems/headphones that are the part of the chain that matters most when it comes to what the music sounds like. Like many misconceptions sources do play a big part of enjoying how you hear your music so to say iems are most important is true but in my personal opinion sources play an equal part in shaping the sound and enjoyment so both can be true at the same time, yes the iem/headphone are the majority stake holder when to comes to what we hear but the source finesses the nuances and nuances are often the difference between a $400 and a $4K iem. So nuances are important.

So I personally, my insanely large collection of sources all have an influence on my enjoyment and I strongly believe in synergy, how a source and even a cable helps to sculpture the sound and enjoyment of my music library with specific combinations.

I'm not the type of reviewer that gets deep in to the weeds expounding on the absolute differences between components and technical jargon. I am not a technician, I'm a guy obsessed with music and enjoying it with the type of gear that makes it sound enjoyable and separates me the outside world to my happy place.

I have very expensive sources and cheap as chips budget sources both can stand equal in my enjoyment. You really can exist in this hobby by living squarely in the budget arena. Now I hear the outcry already why would you buy the expensive gear then? Well there are absolute differences and quantifiable improvements as you climb the ladder but does that equate to more enjoyment? That depends on subjective experience.

I had zero issues I with the Magic Sound 5LE with Bluetooth or wired via usb no drivers were required for connection to android devices or Microsoft windows it was a seamless experience.

Battery life from the 2500mAh battery was surprisingly good getting a solid 9hours from Bluetooth and 8 and a bit from wired using the 4.4mm balanced add to this the18w fast charging which takes roughly 40min to fully charge makes for an extremely useful addition to my on the road audio life. 

Iems I used with the Acmee Magic Sound 5LE

Tinhifi P2Ultra a remarkable planar iem thats a pig to drive Tinhifi recommend 3watts of power. While the Magic Sound 5LE didn't manage to squeeze all the potential out of this small 12mm planar with it's nearly 1Watt of power it managed to present 85 out of 100 of their potential. The P2Ultra's authtorive bottom end with lush mids showed excellent natural staging in all directions and most importantly those treble edges are smoothed out while keeping detail and sparkle. They were plenty loud enough to play at unhealthy volume.

AFUL Dawn-X this flagship iem from AFUL has quite the unique sound especially when it comes to holographic staging. The Dawn-X was wonderfully suited the textured bass was rendered with expert ease with no bloom or lingering transients the open airy mids while having a natural body didn’t lose that emotional connection pianos, guitars and strings had clear defined edges with very natural transients nothing lingering longer than it should. The upper mids and treble are super clean and weighted with a natural crisp snappy authority that's never harsh or showing unwanted sibilance. I enjoyed the Dawn-X with the Magic Sound 5LE more than the Fiio BTR17 which has a colder more digital playback the synergy with the Magic Sound 5LW was strong with this pairing.

UA ZhuanXu this remarkable iem from the home of Venture Electronics when used with the correct source is a giant killer rubbing shoulders with the Kilobuck elites

The Magic Sound 5LE is such a device the way the bass is expanded and given extra thump and rumble is breathtaking the intro to Another one bites the dust by Queen is a visceral experience as that famous bass line draws us into the groove. This has rarely sounded so commanding from a device in this price bracket. The clarity of Freddie's vocals as you can pick up his vocal chords straining is as if a veil has been lifted. The ZhuanXu was equally at home with Metallica and Orchestra with the clean separation and open staging . This is of course while using the Magic Sound 5LE connected via usb cable. Something that I really appricate is the complete lack of interference when the Magic Sound 5LE Is attached to the back of my phone.

I have to say upfront I'm Bluetooth phobic but in the spirit of fairness I gave it a try and yes it works sounds OK/Good with rock solid connection. It supports high resolution codecs and works as advertised. I suggest you look around at other reviewers impressions as its a function i won't be using again I'm team wired all the way.

Comparison

The Fiio Btr 17 is and has been my favoured Bluetooth dongle for quite a while with its desktop power capability and small form factor, chunky candy bar design. This design is also its downfall when compared to the Magic Sound 5LE while fastened to the back of my phone its was awkward to slide in and out of my pocket. When it comes to the most important aspect, sound the Btr17 is a more analytical digital rendition which is impressive I do find a touch fatiguing. The Magic Sound 5LE on the other hand has an analogue, organic playback that sound smooth and musical which I can use for hours without any fatigue. The extra power that the Magic Sound possess without need to plug in a separate power source is far more practical for life on the move. The Btr17 is a capable dongle and the difference in sound signatures are subjective so choose which suits you taste you can't really go wrong with either, or join me in my need to have choices in my collection to suit my mood.

Conclusion

The Acmee Sound 5LE is a refined and superbly well-built portable Bluetooth DAC/AMP that will fit into your portable life any number of places around your music sources, enabling enjoyment of streaming from your mobile phone, laptop and tablets.

The Acmee Magic Sound 5LE is well designed and pocketable piece of kit that sounds incredible and has enough power to satisfy. I Highly recommend the Acmee Magic Sound 5LE.
