### Post #2 by **Member** ()

To kick start it, I will share my newly acquired GEC 807 ATS25 as power tubes in the Super Gold UltimatE. Drivers are 6S3P-DR and Tesla EZ81 rectifier. Caps used are Mundorf Mcap Supreme S/G/O.

I have always regarded my Telefunken EL12 spez, EL39, Philips Miniwatt EL34 metal base and Philips Miniwatt EL34 double D getters brown base as the best of the EL series. Whilst the GEC 807 is just one day into burn in, I regard the tone to be the equal of the other 4. Different of course but equally good. Most prominent is midrange and midbass. Lovely texture in the DNA of GEC tubes.

My 2nd pair of Gec Kt63 which I also bought is on the way away, which will probably only reach me next week.

6BQ7A tubes arrive tomorrow, I'm curious to see how the Hitachi and Haltron do.

At the moment the EL34 tubes are still running in the Ultigreg and in the other amplifier KT88.

Both very good despite burn in.

With both pairings I can actually hear minimal differences from the Gustard R26 Dac when it is either running via Lan or I2S connected to the Singxer Su 2.

Via Lan it is clearer, fuller, somewhat brighter and nicely powerful.

With the SU2 there is a little less of this but more quietness, it seems a little tidier, a touch warmer and not quite as resolving.

I never noticed this before, but I have for a few days now.
