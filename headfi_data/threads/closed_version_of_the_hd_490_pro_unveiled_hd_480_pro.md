HD 480 PRO technical data

Acoustic principle: closed

Ear coupling: circumaural

Transducer principle: dynamic

Transducer diameter: 38 mm

Frequency response: 3 to 28,700 Hz (-10 dB)

Sensitivity: 107 dB SPL (at 1 kHz/1Vrms); 98 dB (at 1 kHz, 1 mW)

Max. SPL: 130 dB (1 kHz, 5% THD)

THD: <0.5% (at 1 kHz, 100 dB SPL)

Impedance: 130 ohms (1 kHz)

Power rating: 300 mW (100 h, noise as per IEC 60268)

Temperature range: 0°C to +50°C for operation; -25°C to +70°C for storage

Relative humidity: 10 to 80%, non-condensing for operation; 10 to 90% for storage

Weight: 272 g (w/o cable)
Available accessories

Replacement recording ear pads, replacement coiled cable (3 m), 1.8 m straight cable, 3 m straight cable, balanced cable, (replacement) travel case, (storage) case, (replacement) bag.

The HD 480 PRO retails at USD $479 (MSRP). It features recording earpads and a 9 foot coiled cable and is delivered complete with a carrying bag. Instead of the bag, the HD 480 PRO Plus comes with a travel case, and retails at USD $519 (MSRP).

Different companies (Sonova for HDB 630 and Neumann for HD 480 Pro). When Sennheiser Consumer was spun off, the Professional division only retained joint custody of some of the IP (see: HD 400 Pro, which is the same as the 560S). Any new products developed by either would not be available to the other. As the HD 490 Pro chassis was a new product developed by Sennheiser Professional, Consumer was not able to use it for the HDB 630.
