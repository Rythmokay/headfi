### Post #1 by **Gold Fox** ()

If you love CDs but wish they were a little more 
portable
, we'd like to introduce something we've been working on—the new Cayin CP6.

CP6 brings Cayin's signature tube CD sound into a portable form factor, so you can enjoy your CD collection without being tied to a desktop setup. And it's much more than just a CD player. It also works as a USB DAC, Bluetooth transmitter/receiver, and even supports lossless CD ripping, making it surprisingly versatile for everyday use.

For outputs, you get both 3.5mm and 4.4mm headphone jacks, along with line outputs, giving you plenty of flexibility whether you're listening with headphones or integrating CP6 into a larger system.

At the heart of CP6 are two carefully matched 
JAN6418
 NOS tubes, allowing you to switch between Classic Tube, Modern Tube, and Solid-State sound signatures with a single button press. Better yet, all three timbres are available not only through the headphone outputs, but 
also via line out and Bluetooth
, so your favorite sound isn't limited to wired listening.

Power is equally flexible. CP6 features dual DC/BAT operating modes. When powered by DC, the balanced headphone output delivers up to 
1300mW
 (4.4mm BAL), while battery mode lets you take your music anywhere. It also supports QC/PD fast charging, so even a power bank can keep the music going during longer listening sessions.

Wireless users aren't left out, either. Powered by Qualcomm's QCC3083 with Bluetooth 5.4, CP6 supports both Bluetooth transmission and reception—and yes, all three timbre modes remain 
fully functional
 even over Bluetooth.

Whether you're rediscovering your CD collection, building a compact desktop setup, or simply looking for a portable player with a little more personality, we think CP6 has plenty to offer.
