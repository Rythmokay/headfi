### Post #1 by **tassardar** ()

Notices this local brand selling a full R2R DAC.

LAiV Harmony

My initial impression was its pretty good when it comes to soundstage, atmosphere, bass impact. Things just sound bigger and well spread out while having some additional top smoothness that reduces sibilance for me when testing with Austrian Audio Composer.

Will probably be using it for the next few days and do a full review. Done a unboxing and initial impression of this DAC. Only compared it with a M17 but the next time I'll setup my Hugo TT for a proper comparison.

And it's really solid built. I actually kinda like touching it haha.

### Post #8 by **Member** ()

I will likely be able to compare to a cyan 2 in 2 weeks time but if it’s say versus a Hugo TT, it has a tighter bass, a touch less depth, it is more mellow, alittle warmer, bigger sounding, smoother in the mids and the highs but less immediate. 

I only have R2R dongles and it’s definitely better than those, mainly in sound stage and overall smoothness and control. 

Don’t think it answers anyone question if compared to a denafrips or holo audio, both of which I’ll probably get access in 2-3 weeks time.
