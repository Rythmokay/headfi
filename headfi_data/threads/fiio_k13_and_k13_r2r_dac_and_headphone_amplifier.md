Fully differential true 24Bit R2R resistor arrays DAC
​
The K13 R2R uses FIIO’s proprietary four-channel fully differential 24-bit R2R resistor array DAC, which is composed of 48 precision thin-film resistors per channel, for a total of 192 resistors across four channels. Each resistor features 0.1% accuracy and low temperature drift (30ppm). This R2R DAC allows the K13 R2R to deliver a uniquely rich and musical sound. In addition, the NOS (Non-Oversampling) and OS (Oversampling) modes bring two different listening experiences.
Preamplifier + power amplifier output, desktop decoding hub
​
With a wide variety of inputs and outputs, the K13 R2R functions as both a preamplifier and a power amplifier, catering to various listening preferences. It also supports PCM 384kHz/32bit and DSD256 decoding, allowing it to effortlessly process high-resolution audio signals to deliver rich sonic detail and for an outstanding listening experience.
Specially-designed power supply
​
The K13 R2R is designed with DC/AC dual power supplies. The built-in 30W switching power supply is protected by an alloy nickel-plated shielding cover, which significantly boosts its anti-interference capabilities and ensures a cleaner power supply. Additionally, it also features an external DC power input, allowing you to pair it with a higher-performance linear power supply for a different sound experience.

What’s more, the internal power supply materials never compromise. With up to 18 low-noise LDOs and 5 DC-DC converters in total, this unit employs a multi-rail power supply to provide strong and clean power for exceptional audio output. Premium ELNA RA3 series silk-film audio capacitors and specialized audio tantalum electrolytic capacitors work together to ensure a pure, stable power supply for the device, delivering a warm and natural sound.

For more impressive features, such as the fully balanced audio amplifier circuit, 2400mW+2400mW powerful output, LDAC Bluetooth, 10-band high-precision PEQ, adjustable RGB lighting, etc., please visit the product page on our website.

Answer from Google
:  For high-quality audio, a well-designed DC power supply is preferable to AC for the internal components because audio circuits require stable, low-noise DC power, which can be achieved with a high-quality power supply. While most home audio equipment receives AC power from the wall, it must be converted to DC internally by the power supply. The quality of the power supply's internal components, its ability to produce a stable and noise-free DC output, and its transient response are more important for audio quality than whether the input is AC or DC.
