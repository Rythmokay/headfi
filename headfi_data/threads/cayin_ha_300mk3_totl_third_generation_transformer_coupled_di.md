Cayin HA-300MK3

The Cayin HA-300 MK3 represents the third-generation evolution of Cayin’s 300B headphone amplifier platform, refining both circuit architecture and industrial design. Building upon the acclaimed MK2, the MK3 introduces a fully upgraded wide-bandwidth transformer system, improved power delivery topology, and enhanced compatibility with headphones—from sensitive IEMs to demanding high-impedance full-size designs.

At its core, the HA-300 MK3 remains a purist single-ended Class A triode amplifier, powered by a matched pair of Genalex Gold Lion PX300B tubes, driven by 6SN7 voltage amplification, and supported by a newly optimized GZ34/5AR4 based rectification and power supply system. With expanded output capability, lower noise floor, and improved component quality, the MK3 delivers a more resolving, controlled, and immersive listening experience.

The Heart of the MK3: Third-Generation Transformer Architecture​
If the 300B tubes define the soul of the HA-300 MK3, then the newly developed transformer system is unquestionably its backbone.

For this generation, Cayin engineered a 
third-generation wide-bandwidth EI output transformer
 alongside a 
custom toroidal power transformer
, both specifically optimized for this platform. Compared to previous designs, these transformers deliver:
Significantly improved electromagnetic shielding
Lower power ripple and noise floor
Greater signal integrity across the frequency spectrum
More stable and cleaner power delivery
In practical terms, this translates to a presentation that is not only more transparent and controlled, but also more spatially coherent—preserving the harmonic richness of 300B while improving extension, layering, and dynamic precision.

In Cayin, we treat the transformer as an active tuning component. Every aspect—from core material selection to winding structure and bandwidth optimization—is engineered to balance musicality with technical performance. This is especially critical in a transformer-coupled SET design, where the output transformer directly shapes the amplifier’s tonal density, staging, and transient behavior.
​
The Design Story: Preserving Tradition While Expanding Capability​
The HA-300 MK3 is not a departure from Cayin’s philosophy—it is a continuation, refined through iteration.

Cayin has long been committed to 
single-ended triode (SET) topology
, valuing its simplicity and direct signal path. However, as headphone ecosystems evolved—especially with the rise of high-sensitivity IEMs and diverse impedance loads—the limitations of traditional SET implementations became more apparent.

The MK3 addresses this not by abandoning tradition, but by 
re-engineering the supporting architecture around it
:
A physically separated dual-chassis design with 
improved EMI and noise isolations,
 reducing interference at the structural level
A 
low-noise regulated multi-stage power delivery system
 ensures each amplification stage receives stable, clean energy
Output power and sensitivity are expanded to accommodate a much wider range of headphones
The 4.4mm balanced output is
 specifically optimized
 for low-noise, low-power IEM use—something rarely seen in classic 300B amplifiers
At the same time, Cayin retains traditional craftsmanship through 
point-to-point hand wiring
, carefully selected matched tubes, and extensive burn-in procedures. This reflects a deliberate balance: modern usability layered onto a fundamentally analog, artisanal core.

In essence, the HA-300 MK3 is the result of a clear engineering narrative—not chasing specifications alone, but solving real-world usability challenges while preserving the emotional and tonal identity that defines a 300B SET amplifier.
